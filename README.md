# Le jardin de Melina

Private educational app for Melina (2e année du primaire, Québec), with two kinds of activity:

- **On-screen mini-games**, with art, sound and voice. She plays on her own and the answer is checked immediately. There are eight: *Les caisses de pommes* (groups of 10), *Le train des nombres* (sequences), *Saute-grenouille* (addition and subtraction), *La pêche aux mots* (plural), *L'histoire mystère* (reading), *Le chemin des nombres* (counting accurately up to 100, including on the hundred grid), *Les mots cachés* (word search with accents and one word to complete at the end) and *Les dominos des nombres* (the same number as dots, digits, sums, and tens and ones). All of them use a fixed stage that keeps the pieces in place at any window size.
- **Paper activities** (*Le jardin de Lila*): she answers on the sheet, the parents photograph or type the answer and confirm what is written. The AI (Claude) is optional and only helps to **read** the handwriting.

- The whole app is in **Canadian French**, including the parent area and the art studio (since 17/11/2026). This documentation is in English.
- It runs only on this computer: the address is `http://127.0.0.1:3001` and the app does not accept connections from other machines.
- The data lives in `data\`: the SQLite database and the photos.
- The app works without Internet and without an API key, using manual transcription.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
- Nothing else: the Node.js the app uses is already inside the folder, in `runtime\node.exe` (version 24.22.3, portable). Nothing is installed on Windows.

The origin and checksum of `node.exe` are in `runtime\LEIA-ME.txt`. If you prefer, delete the `runtime` folder and install Node.js LTS (22.13 or newer) from <https://nodejs.org>: `iniciar.bat` uses whatever it finds, first in `runtime`, then in Windows.

## Opening

1. Double-click **`iniciar.bat`**.
   - It looks for Node.js (first in the `runtime` folder, then in Windows), starts the app and opens the browser at `http://127.0.0.1:3001`.
   - If the app is already open in another window, it only opens the browser.
2. To quit, close the black window or press `Ctrl+C` in it.

From the terminal, opened in the `C:\MelinaApp` folder:

```bat
runtime\node.exe apps\server\dist\server.mjs
```

**For whoever changes the code** (optional): with Node.js and pnpm installed, `pnpm install`, `pnpm build` (rebuilds the interface and the server) and `pnpm dev` (development mode). `instalar.bat` does the install and the build.

## How to use

**Mini-games (on screen)**
1. On the home screen, she clicks the game card.
2. In *Les caisses de pommes*, she clicks the fruits (or presses the space bar) to arrange them in boxes of 10 and then answers on the number pad. In the train, she types the missing number; in the frog, fish and story games, she clicks the answer. In the number path, she taps an empty stone and types; the correct stones turn green after a mistake. In the hidden words, she taps the first and last letter of each word and, at the end, completes the word she hears. In the dominoes, she taps the piece that shows the same number as the free end.
3. If she gets it wrong, she receives a hint and tries again; on the third mistake, the game shows the answer with the explanation. There is never any punishment.
4. The app picks the level by itself and records everything in the parent dashboard.

**Art du jeu (parent area, *Espace parents*)**
In the *Art du jeu* tab you replace the scenery, the objects and the character with images generated by an image API or taken from ready-made packs. Nothing goes into the game without your approval. The step-by-step guide is in [`docs/visual-architecture.md`](docs/visual-architecture.md).

The app opens in **Microsoft Edge**, in its own window, because only Edge has the natural Canadian French voice (it needs Internet). Without Internet, or in another browser, the game uses the Windows voice, if there is a French one. The speaker button turns sound and voice on and off. To use the default browser, see `MELINA_BROWSER` in `.env.example`.

**Melina — paper activities**
1. Click *Continuer l'aventure*. She walks with the arrow keys or W A S D and talks with Espace or E. She can also click on the places.
2. She talks to Lila, the hérissonne. The star shows the next place.
3. She reads the exercise, writes the answer **on the sheet** and clicks *J'ai terminé sur papier*. If she wants to give up, *J’arrête* (with confirmation) cancels the activity without counting it as a mistake; in a mission, the step comes back later with another exercise.

**Parents** (the *Espace parents* button, which asks a multiplication question)
1. In *Vérifier les réponses*, choose the photo of the answer or click *Saisir la réponse à la main*.
2. If you like, use *Lire avec l’IA*.
3. **Confirm exactly what is written**, even if it is wrong, and indicate whether she had help solving it.
4. Click *Montrer le résultat à Melina*. She sees the feedback and the mission moves on.

The parent dashboard also shows the history, the skills, the upcoming reviews, the handwriting library, the AI settings and the backup.

> The multiplication question only keeps the child from getting in by accident. **It is not a password** and it does not protect the data.

## Enabling AI reading (optional)

1. Copy `.env.example` to `.env`, in the same folder.
2. Fill in `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=` with your key. **Never paste the key into messages or e-mails.**
3. Check the model in `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`. The default is `claude-sonnet-5-5`.
4. Restart the app. In *Espace parents → IA et données*, read the notice and turn it on.

Each reading sends the photo of the answer and, if you allow it, a few handwriting samples. Each reading may incur a cost on the API account. Details in [`docs/privacy.md`](docs/privacy.md).

## Where the data lives

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Database (history, skills, missions) | `data\melina.db` (with `-wal` and `-shm` next to it while the app is open) |
| Answer photos | `data\photos\` (names generated by the app) |
| Backups | `data\backups\backup-...\` |
| API key | `.env` (never goes into the backup) |

**Backup:** use *Espace parents → IA et données → Faire une sauvegarde maintenant*. Before a database migration, the app also makes a backup by itself.

**Restore:** close the app. Copy `melina.db` from the backup to `data\melina.db` and the `photos` folder to `data\photos`. Then open the app.

Local storage **is not encrypted**. Protect the Windows account with a password.

## Tests

They require the development environment (Node.js + pnpm + `pnpm install`):

```bat
pnpm test        :: 112 automated tests (rules, API, persistence, stage, art, simulated AI)
pnpm typecheck
```

The browser tests need Python with Playwright (on Windows, the environment lives in `.venv` and uses the installed Edge). They always use a temporary data folder, never `data\`:

```bat
set MELINA_NODE=runtime\node.exe
.venv\Scripts\python tests\e2e\run_e2e.py       :: full flow (paper, 5 mini-games, studio)
.venv\Scripts\python tests\e2e\layout_check.py  :: stage alignment at 10 window sizes
```

Results in [`docs/testing.md`](docs/testing.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Node.js was not found" | The `runtime` folder was deleted or is missing `node.exe`. Install Node.js LTS from nodejs.org or put `node.exe` back in `runtime\`. |
| "The Node.js found is too old" | Update to the current LTS version. |
| "Port 3001 is being used by another program" | Create or edit `.env` with `PORT=3002` and open again. |
| The browser did not open by itself | Open `http://127.0.0.1:3001` manually. |
| "Application files not found" | The folder is incomplete: `apps\server\dist` or `apps\game\dist` is missing. |
| "Lire avec l’IA" disabled | The key is missing in `.env` or it has not been turned on in *IA et données*. Manual transcription keeps working. |
| The AI failed or took too long | Type the answer manually. The failure does not count as Melina's mistake. |
| Photo rejected | Use JPEG, PNG or WebP. The app reduces the size automatically. |
