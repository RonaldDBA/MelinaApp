## Teaching observations (summary, no copying)

### Math — "La récolte de citrouilles" (`Scanned document (10).pdf`, pp. 2–3)
- Task: count a large drawn collection (dozens of objects) and work out how many boxes of
  at most 10 are needed (fill one before starting another).
- Melina's answer × marking (p. 2): first collection **36** (correct: 37); boxes **4** (accepted).
- Melina's answer × marking (p. 3): second collection **94** (correct: 89); boxes **9** (accepted).
- **Teaching reading:** the reasoning "groups of 10 → number of boxes, including the
  incomplete box" is there. The observed difficulty is **accuracy when counting large,
  disorganized collections** (forgetting or repeating items; the drawn groupings overlap).
- **Consequence in the app:** prioritize organized counting by groups of 10, tens/ones,
  and "how many boxes of 10?" problems (round up). Skills `math.place-value`
  and `math.grouping-tens`.

### French — "Le temps des pommes" (images 22_07_08 to 22_08_00)
- Questionnaire on explicit information (who, what, where, when) + justified opinion.
- All explicit answers accepted by the teacher.
- In the opinion answer, the writing is phonetic and without accents (something like "c'était drôle" written
  approximately). It points to a focus on **spelling of frequent words and accents**.
- Handwriting: in some places the **n** can look like an **h** (e.g. in the character's surname).
  Relevant for image reading: n/h confusions, joined letters and irregular spacing.

### French — material prepared at home (`Melina_Dia1_...pdf`, `Evaluation_Une_fete...pdf`)
- A format that works at home: short 5-word dictation, fill in the blanks with a word bank,
  rewrite a sentence fixing capital letter/full stop/spelling, comprehension questions answered in a
  complete sentence, checklist "Majuscule / Point final / Orthographe".
- This format inspired the exercise types `fill-blank`, `rewrite-sentence` and `comprehension`.

### Commercial book (`Scanned document(2).pdf`)
- Used only to understand the reading level (short sentences, familiar vocabulary, proper
  names). **Characters, text and illustrations are not used in the app** (copyright).
