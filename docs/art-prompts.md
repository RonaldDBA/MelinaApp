# Prompts for the realistic images, per game

To generate **outside the app** and put in `assets\packs-inbox\`. The same cards can also be generated **inside the app**,
in the studio (*Espace parents → Art du jeu*), which already uses OpenAI with the key from `.env`.

How to hand them to the app:
1. Create one folder per game inside `assets\packs-inbox\`, for example `assets\packs-inbox\chemin-des-nombres\`.
2. Save the images as **PNG with a transparent background** (or WebP), at the indicated size or larger, in the same aspect ratio.
3. Put a `LICENSE.txt` in the folder with one line saying where they came from (e.g. «Generated by us with model X on 17/11/2026»).
4. In the app: *Espace parents → Art du jeu → Chercher des paquets*, open the card and choose the image. Nothing goes into the game without this approval.

The prompts are in English because the generators follow English better. All of them ask for realistic photography, with no text and no people,
consistent with the app's style guide («Verger de Lila — photographie»).

## Le chemin des nombres

### Path stone — card `path.stone`
Size: **1024×1024**, PNG with a transparent background. Without an approved photo, the game keeps drawing the stone.

> Top-down product photograph of a single real flat stepping stone, rounded and slightly oval, light grey granite with fine natural speckles, smooth dry surface, soft rounded edges, a subtle natural shadow only on the stone itself. The center of the stone is clean, light and even, because a large number will be printed on top of it. Centered, the whole stone visible with free margin all around, isolated on a fully transparent background. Soft natural morning light from the upper right, sharp focus, true-to-life colors. No moss, no grass, no soil, no other stones, no large cracks, no markings, no letters, no numbers, no text, no watermark.

### Finish basket — card `path.basket`
Size: **1024×1024**, PNG with a transparent background. Without an approved photo, the current basket icon is used.

> Photograph of a real woven wicker basket with a single arched handle, filled to the top with fresh red McIntosh apples with a few green leaves, seen from the front and slightly above, the whole basket and handle in frame, centered with free margin all around, isolated on a fully transparent background. Soft natural morning light from the upper right, realistic wicker and apple skin textures, sharp focus, true-to-life colors. No people, no hands, no table, no cloth, no ground, no labels, no price tags, no text, no watermark.

Tip: after approving the stone, check in the game that the numbers are still easy to read. The app puts each number on a white badge when the stone is a photo.

## Les mots cachés

### Word-search sheet — card `hidden.sheet`
Size: **1024×1024**, PNG with a transparent background. Without an approved photo, the game keeps drawing the sheet. The grid letters are written on top: the sheet must be light and smooth.

> Top-down photograph of a single real sheet of plain school notebook paper, square, off-white with a very subtle cream tone, flat, clean and smooth, with a soft natural paper texture and straight clean edges, no visible ruled lines. The sheet fills almost the whole frame with a small free margin, isolated on a fully transparent background. Soft even natural daylight, no strong shadows, sharp focus. No writing, no letters, no numbers, no drawings, no stains, no folds, no tears, no pencil, no desk, no objects around, no watermark.

### Magnifying glass — card `hidden.loupe`
Size: **1024×1024**, PNG with a transparent background. It appears on the home-screen card and in the word list.

> Photograph of a real reading magnifying glass with a dark metal rim and a light natural wooden handle, tilted diagonally, clean clear glass lens with a soft natural reflection, the whole object visible, centered with free margin all around, isolated on a fully transparent background. Soft natural morning light from the upper right, realistic metal, glass and wood textures, sharp focus. No hands, no people, no paper or letters seen through the lens, no table, no background objects, no logos, no text, no watermark.

## Les dominos des nombres

### Wooden table — card `domino.table` (game scenery)
Size: **1536×864**, **opaque** PNG or JPEG (no transparency). Without an approved photo, the game uses the orchard scenery.

> Top-down photograph of a real light-colored wooden kitchen table top in a Québec country house, smooth surface with natural wood grain and a few subtle marks of use, softly lit by gentle morning light coming from a window on the right. The table top fills the entire frame edge to edge and is completely empty. Realistic, sharp, warm natural colors. No objects on the table, no people, no hands, no tablecloth, no table edges, no floor, no text, no watermark.

### Blank domino tile — card `domino.tile`
Size: **1536×768**, PNG with a transparent background. Without an approved photo, the game keeps drawing the tiles. The game draws the dots, numbers and sums on top: **both halves must be empty**.

> Top-down photograph of a single real large domino tile lying horizontally, ivory-colored, smooth slightly glossy surface, rounded corners, a raised dividing line across the middle and a small brass pin at its center. Both halves are completely blank, with no pips, no numbers and no markings. The whole tile visible, centered with a small free margin, isolated on a fully transparent background. Soft natural light, subtle realistic shading, sharp focus. No other tiles, no table, no background, no strong shadow, no text, no watermark.
