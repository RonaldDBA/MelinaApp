# Privacy and data

## What stays on the computer

Everything stays inside `C:\MelinaApp\data\`: the local profile (only the first name "Melina" and the school year), the photos, the answers, the history, the skills, the handwriting samples and the settings.

- There is no online account, telemetry, trackers, ads or CDN.
- The server only accepts connections from the computer itself.
- **Local storage is not encrypted.** Anyone with access to the Windows account can read `data\`. Protect the account with a password.
- The app does not ask for or keep the full name, school or date of birth.

## Players (since 18/11/2026)

- «Qui joue?» comes before the home screen. Each player (up to 8) has their own history, levels, flowers, garden,
  reports and model; a friend starts from zero and never sees or changes Melina's data.
- A player is a first name and an animal (no photo, no age, no school). Creating one goes through the parents' gate.
- The settings that belong to a child are per player: the parents' next choice, the school week and the **consent to
  read handwriting with the AI** (given for Melina only; another player starts without it).
- The name is shown on screen only. It is never spoken by the voice and never sent outside the computer.

## What can leave the computer

Only with AI reading turned on, which requires accepting the notice in *IA et données*, and only when the parents click **Lire avec l’IA**:

| Sent to Anthropic (Claude API) | Not sent |
|---|---|
| The photo of the answer, already stripped of metadata | Name, school, age |
| Up to N **authorized** handwriting samples (default 3) with the confirmed transcriptions | Exercise question |
| The answer type (e.g. "un nombre") | Expected answer, history, skills |

Each reading may incur a cost on the API account.

## Mini-game voice

The mini-games speak the instructions with the browser's speech synthesis. In Microsoft Edge, the natural Canadian French voice is an online Microsoft service: the **text of the spoken sentence** (for example, "Combien de pommes y a-t-il en tout?") is sent to generate the audio. No data about the child, answers or history goes with it. To avoid this service, turn the sound off with the speaker button or use the app without Internet (Windows voice).

## Photos

- The interface fixes the orientation and creates a new reduced image, without EXIF and without location.
- The server validates the file's real content and **removes the metadata again**: EXIF, XMP and GPS in JPEG and WebP, and text chunks in PNG.
- The file name is generated by the app. The name sent by the browser is ignored.
- The parents should photograph **only the answer**, with no faces and no other pages.

## API key

- It lives only in the `.env` file, read by the server. It never goes to the browser, the logs, the backup or version control (`.gitignore`).
- Do not paste the key into messages or shared files.

## Deletion

| Action | What is deleted | What remains |
|---|---|---|
| Delete a handwriting sample | The sample from the library. It is no longer used by the AI. | The attempt, the confirmed transcription and the photo of the answered exercise |
| Cancel an exercise | The open readings | The exercise record as "cancelled" |
| Delete everything | Close the app and delete the `data\` folder. Make a backup first, if you want. | Nothing |

There is no deletion of individual photos through the interface yet. It is planned in `docs/roadmap.md`.

## Backup

- The backup copies the database consistently (`VACUUM INTO`, safe with WAL) and the referenced photos.
- It **does not include** `.env`.
- The app makes an automatic backup before database migrations.
